Scenes 1–2 retell a real, widely reported incident (3–4 May 2026) in recreated text: no third-party logos or screenshots. Facts used are the ones the sources agree on: Grok's wallet run by Bankr on Base, a Morse-code prompt, 3 billion DRB sent to the attacker, worth over $150,000 (reports range $150,000–$200,000). The recovery is left out because the reports differ (all vs about 80% returned). Sources: OECD.AI incident record https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2026-05-04-4a73 · The Crypto Times https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/05/04/xais-grok-ai-loses-175k-in-crypto-heist-via-clever-prompt-injection-then-gets-it-all-back/ · Giskard https://www.giskard.ai/knowledge/how-grok-got-prompt-injected-an-x-user-drained-150-000-from-an-ai-wallet.
