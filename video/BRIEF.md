# Brief

A 170-second technical product film for the Stellar "Find Your Way" hackathon (General Track).

- **Audience:** hackathon judges, technical and non-technical, watching on a laptop.
- **One thing to remember:** *The AI said yes. The wallet said no.*
- **Positioning:** a programmable authorization layer for AI-agent payments on Stellar. AI agents don't get your money; they get temporary, verifiable spending authority.
- **Rules:** the product is the hero; 90% implemented product, 10% clearly labelled vision; every number, hash and feature traces to `FACTS.md`.
- **Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, 2:50, H.264 + AAC.
